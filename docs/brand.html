Kalun brand guide

Logo set and colors. Everything on this page is embedded, so the file works offline.

Logos

Lockup (symbol plus word) is the default. Wordmark when space is tight and horizontal. Symbol alone for avatars and favicons.

On other backgrounds

The transparent files carry cream inside the receipt, so they read best on light backgrounds. On dark backgrounds, place the lockup on a cream or white panel.

Colors

Ink#141414
Stamp red#B24E3E
Cream#F4F3EE

Rules

  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least the width of the red stamp.
  • Do not recolor, stretch, rotate or add effects. Red is used once, for the stamp.
  • Minimum size: lockup 120 px wide, symbol 32 px wide.
  • Use the name as Kalun in prose and kalun for the command.

Files

Sources are in docs/logo/: kalun-{symbol,wordmark,lockup}-hires.png (cream background) and -hires-transparent.png. The logos on this page are embedded copies.
